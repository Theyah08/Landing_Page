PixoPet, Your Pixel Pet

Choose a pet. Build its home. Watch it grow.

PixoPet is a pixel-style virtual pet game where you can choose your own home pet, take care of it every day, decorate its room, and help it grow through fun activities. Feed, play, and customize your pet while enjoying a relaxing retro pixel experience designed for casual players and pet lovers of all ages.

Key Features

Choose Your Pet

Select from cats, dogs, birds, and more cute pixel pets.

Decorate Their Room

Customize your pet’s personal room with furniture and colors.

Care & Level Up

Feed, clean, and play to help your pet grow stronger.

About PixoPet

PixoPet is inspired by classic virtual pet games. It allows players to experience caring for a pet in a fun pixel world.

The game is relaxing, simple, and perfect for casual gamers and pet lovers.
